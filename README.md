## About

**Pulse for Todoist** brings your [Todoist](https://todoist.com) tasks to your Garmin watch so you can stay on top of your day from the wrist. See what's due, check tasks off, reschedule with a tap, and dig into the details. All on the watch!

It keeps a local cache and **queues your changes when you're offline**, syncing automatically the moment your phone is back in range. Pick your default view, sort order, and more from the settings.

*Unofficial, third-party app. Requires a Todoist account. Not affiliated with Doist — see [Disclaimer](#disclaimer).*

## Features

- **Multiple views** — Today, Overdue, Today + Overdue, Upcoming (configurable window).
- **Complete tasks**. You can complete tasks from your watch and next time you are online they are synced to Todoist. 
- **Quick reschedule** — Later Today, Tomorrow, Next Week, or +30 days from the actions menu.
- **Add tasks** — type a task in plain language and Todoist's Quick Add parses dates and `@labels` (on touchscreen devices with the on-screen keyboard, e.g. Venu / FR970).
- **Rich task details** — title, due date, project, description, and labels.
- **Smart subtasks** — subtasks that match your current view (e.g. due today) appear as their own rows with a breadcrumb to their parent task.
- **Flexible sorting** — by Due Date, Priority (highest first), or Alphabetically.
- **Works offline** — completions and reschedules are queued and synced automatically on reconnect.
- **Helpful empty state** — when your list is clear, optionally surface tomorrow's or no-due-date tasks.
- **Glance support** — compact task count + progress on glance-capable devices.
- **Automatic + manual sync** — background sync every few minutes, plus instant manual refresh (long-press the up button).
- **Configurable** in Garmin Connect Mobile.

## Install

1. **[Get Pulse for Todoist on the Connect IQ Store](CONNECT_IQ_LINK)** and install it to your watch via Garmin Connect Mobile.
2. Open the app and connect your Todoist account.
For end users (installed from the Connect IQ Store)
2. Get your Todoist API token. Go to todoist.com → Settings → Integrations → Developer (direct link: https://todoist.com/app/settings/integrations/developer).
Copy the API token shown there (a long alphanumeric string). This is a personal token tied to your account, treat it like a password.
3. Go to Connect IQ on your phone and paste it into the app settings.

4. Set your preferred default view and sorting.

## 🔒 Privacy

Pulse for Todoist talks directly to the Todoist API to read and update your tasks. Task data is cached **locally on the watch** only to display it and to queue offline changes. The app does not sell or share your data.
